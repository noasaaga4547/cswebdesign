Fun Fact

The highest-grossing anime film of all time, Demon Slayer: Mugen Train, achieved a
historic milestone by dethroning Hollywood's finest during the 2020 global box office
race. It marked the first time in cinema history that a non-Hollywood film topped the
annual worldwide box office, even beating out major American blockbusters.
This monumental success highlights how anime has evolved from a niche Japanese
subculture into a powerhouse of global entertainment. Today, Japan's animation
industry is so massive that it consumes a staggering amount of resources, including a
long-standing urban legend that Japan uses more paper for printing manga than it does
for toilet paper!
