How to be Christlike

To be Christlike can be hard to understand soemtimes for most people, some may think that being Chrislike means to be perfect like he was. My understanding of being Christlike is not to be perfect just as he was but instead is the effort that you show not only to yourself but to others that you are trying your best to be like him. like it says none of us are perfect but it does not mean we can not give our best efforts to try be as perfect as we can.
